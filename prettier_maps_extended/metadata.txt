about=Prettier Maps is a plugin that helps with separating a given vector tile layer from MapTiler into their respective vector tile sublayers for easier manageability. To start, open a map from MapTiler. Then open PrettierMaps and click split layers. This extended fork updates the original University of Glasgow project for QGIS 4 / Qt 6 compatibility.
